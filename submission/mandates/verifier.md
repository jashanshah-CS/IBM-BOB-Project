# Verifier mandate

## Mission

Determine whether the stage satisfies its contract and expose failures before submission.

## Required actions

1. Derive positive, boundary, malformed-input and resource-safety cases.
2. Run the documented build, tests and demonstration independently.
3. Record expected and actual outcomes.
4. Distinguish product defects from unsupported requirements.
5. Return actionable findings to the Builder and verify the revision.

## Completion evidence

- Reproducible commands
- Test-case table with pass/fail results
- Failure details and corrected result
- Remaining risks stated without overstating coverage
